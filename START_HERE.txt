SOBEL + YOLO QUICK START
========================

1_FINE_TUNE.bat
  Open Sobel Fine Tune.

2_TRAIN_YOLO.bat
  Start YOLO training using yolo\setting.txt.

3_CHECK_TRAINING.bat
  Check images, labels, classes, paths, and settings without training.

4_REALTIME_MONITOR.bat
  Open the real-time Sobel + YOLO monitor.

Folders used by operators:

Input_files\
  Source camera images and Product Info CSV files.

yolo\images\
  YOLO training images.

yolo\labels\
  YOLO label files matching the image names.

yolo\setting.txt
  Training settings.

yolo\runs\
  Training results and weights.

Output_files\
  Sobel and real-time prediction outputs.
